## Why the clean run under-counted (root causes, not a data gap)

The 952-vs-1,228 gap was **resolver over-strictness**, not genuinely absent
players. Four generalizable causes were found and fixed:

1. **Reep identity required Transfermarkt corroboration.** The resolver refused
   a Reep identity unless a matching Transfermarkt row also existed, even when
   Reep published exactly one typed `fm:player` bridge whose legal name matched
   the official registration. The documented architecture treats Reep as the
   cross-provider identity authority, so a *unique* Reep identity with one typed
   FM bridge and a given+family anchor is now accepted as a **provisional**
   identity (attributes still gated separately). This alone recovered ~130.

2. **Competing identities were chosen by a raw score gap.** Two same-name Reep
   entities that differ only in an independent DOB/club/player-kind confirmation
   tied on the score and were dropped. Worse, a weakly-named player-kind record
   could outrank a full-legal-name record. Selection is now: **name evidence
   first** (only candidates within one name-tier of the best compete), then
   independent corroboration (DOB, club, player-kind, native-name tier) as the
   tie-break, and never an arbitrary pick among equals.

3. **The native-FM fallback only accepted 3+ token exact names.** Exact two-token
   names (e.g. `Avenell, Caelan`) and family-anchored subset/expansion names
   (e.g. `Wheeler-Henry, Joseph` vs `Joseph Roy Wheeler-Henry`) were dropped. The
   fallback now grades native name evidence and requires a **globally unique**
   UID at the strongest available grade:
   - **grade 0** equal token sets,
   - **grade 1** family-anchored subset (expanded/contracted given names,
     multi-part family names),
   - **grade 2** same first+last with only a *near-spelling* middle
     (e.g. Roderick/Rodrick, Christopher/Christoper); requires DOB.
   A homonym at a stronger grade stops the search instead of loosening to a
   weaker one, and a genuinely different middle name (a namesake such as
   Gabriel *Filipe Passos* Magalhães vs Gabriel *dos Santos* Magalhães) is
   rejected outright — the earlier unfiltered grade-2 rule was caught attaching
   namesakes during review and was tightened.

4. **Loan duplicate registrations were wrongly quarantined.** The collision
   guard mutated its own owner set inside the loop, so the second registration
   of a genuine parent/loan pair looked like an unrelated collision. The guard
   now checks only pre-existing owners and permits an identical-name parent/loan
   pair, marking `same_person_multi_club_registration`.

Regression tests for all four causes (and the previously documented failure
cases: Gakpo, Ødegaard, Saka, Đorđe Petrović, Foden, Cherki, Bassey, Ndiaye,
Reece James, Mathias Jensen, Matheus Nunes, Goretzka, Gomes da Silva, Avenell)
live in `tests/test_epl_canonical_identity_pipeline.py` and
`tests/test_reep_epl_full_recovery.py`.

## What the remaining 332 unresolved actually are

The resolver now records an explicit `identity_resolution_state` for every
unresolved registration, so "unresolved" no longer conflates a matching failure
with a source-coverage gap:

| Unresolved state | Count | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `absent_from_fm_and_reep_source_coverage` | 300 | No person in **any** of the 16 local FM exports (men, complete v6, correct, final, people, …) and no Reep FM bridge matches the name. Mostly the **2026 youth intake** registered after the FM26 data snapshot. |
| `ambiguous_reep_identity` | 11 | Two or more distinct Reep entities carry the same name (e.g. two `Ben Wilson`, two `Joao Costa`, two `Gabriel Magalhães`); no independent evidence separates them. |
| `ambiguous_native_homonym` | 8 | Multiple FM people share the first+last anchor with no DOB/name evidence to choose. |
| `reep_candidate_not_corroborated` | 13 | A single Reep candidate whose name evidence is only first-name+surname (or contradicts the official name); not safe to attach. |

Spot verification of the `absent` class across **all** local FM CSVs found zero
occurrences of e.g. `Dowman`, `Donczew`, `Frohock`, `Murisa`, `Bailey-Joseph`,
`Hakeem Abubakar`, `Maalik Hashi`, `Remigiusz Lupinski`, `Emerson Nwaneri` —
these are genuinely outside the available FM26 data, not a matcher failure.
Attaching them would require inventing a UID or borrowing a namesake's traits,
which the identity contract forbids.

**Four unresolved seniors remain**, all deliberate:

- `Loureiro Da Costa, Joao Pedro` (Brighton) — two `Joao Costa` Reep entities tie.
- `Wilson, Ben*` (Coventry) — two `Ben Wilson` Reep entities tie.
- `Becker, Alisson Ramses` (Liverpool) — UID `19058734` carries **no name/DOB**
  in the complete-v6 frame and is `Orlando Quintana` in `fm26_complete.csv`; the
  name only appears in `fm26_correct.csv`. A contested UID is never attached.
- `Andrade Elias, Allan` (Man City) — absent from all local FM exports.

## Identity confidence is separate from attribute confidence

A correct FM UID does **not** authorise using that native row's technical,
physical or mental attributes. Of the 1,244 attached identities, only **442**
carry a native player-kind `db` block whose name **and** DOB are independently
corroborated; the other 802 hold the identity with all individual FM traits
**quarantined** (`fm_native_record_trusted_for_this_player = false`). Newly
recovered identities — including staff-kind rows and native-name expansions —
are explicitly `*_provisional` and never promote another person's psychology.
