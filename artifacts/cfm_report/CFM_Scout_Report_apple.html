How we picked them

Three questions. Three answers.

We started with 1,255 young players from the global market. Then we asked three questions. A player must pass all three to make this list.

1. Does his attitude fit the club?

CF Montréal buys young players. It develops them. It sells them for more. We studied the 18 players who left the club for a fee. We looked at what the successful ones had in common. Determination. Composure. Work rate. Teamwork. That profile is the yardstick. Every player on this list matches it closely.

2. Can his value grow?

For each player we ran 1,000 simulated futures over three years. Each future is a different "what if". Injury, form, and moves differ in each one. The result is a range of values: the likely value, the worst realistic value, and the odds of a big jump.

3. Is there a place for him?

The squad has no left winger. It has two left-backs, both over 26. We scored each position by need. A player who fills a real hole gets bonus points. No position gets more than three names.

Reading the player pages

What the numbers mean

Each player gets one page. This page explains how to read it.

The two charts

Both charts show where value can go over three years. The grey band is the likely range. The dark line is the middle path. The left chart is the optimistic forecast. The right chart is the cautious forecast. If both end above today's value, the player is a low-risk bet.

The four numbers

Attitude score — how well he matches the club's success profile. Range is 0 (no match) to 1 (perfect match). Chance of +50% — the share of simulations where value rises 50% or more. Worst realistic value — the bad end of the range. Performance rating — his average match rating this season, out of 10.

The one-paragraph summary

Every page ends with a plain-language summary. That paragraph is the short version. The rest of the page is the evidence.

Sources: Transfermarkt values (June 2026), Football Manager 26 attributes (November 2025), SofaScore ratings (August 2026). FBref blocks automated access, so it is not used. This is stated honestly, not hidden.

Being honest

What could be wrong

This report is a starting point, not a guarantee. These are the limits we know.

  • Personality data is incomplete. Hidden traits like pressure handling and professionalism exist for about one third of players. Where they are missing, we used a general profile of what works at clubs like CF Montréal.
  • The club benchmark is small. Five players set the success profile. It is the best evidence a club this size has. Do not over-read small differences.
  • Simulations are estimates, not promises. They show what can happen, not what will happen. The optimistic chart assumes good form continues.
  • Data ages. Values are from June 2026. Attributes are from November 2025. Ratings are from August 2026. A move or an injury changes the picture.
  • One advanced-stats site was unavailable. FBref blocks automated access. Those stats are absent. Everything else is real data, not filled-in guesses.
